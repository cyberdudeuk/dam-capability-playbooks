    Learning journeys Level: Foundation

    Learn the playbooks

    Short modules that build from the basics of the policy to running all three capabilities as one yearly cycle. Each module lists what you will be able to do, the activities, and a quick check of understanding.

    • 11 modules
    • 255 minutes
    • 3 levels

    In brief

    • 11 modules, about 255 minutes in total, at three levels.
    • Each module has outcomes, a short set of activities and a check of understanding.
    • Follow the journey for your role, or pick any module.

    Levels

    • Foundation
      For anyone new to the policy or to a data role.
    • Practitioner
      For people doing the work of a capability day to day.
    • Advanced
      For people running all three capabilities together.

    Modules in full

    M01 · Foundation · 15 minutes

    The DAM Policy - what it asks of you

    Governance Cataloguing Quality
    Activities done0 of 4 done

    By the end you will be able to

    • Explain who the policy applies to and what is in scope
    • Name the core duties: identify and catalogue assets, report them, name an owner and manage quality
    • Find the requirement behind any step in these playbooks

    Activities

    1. Read. Read the summary of who the policy applies to and what is in scope.
    2. Read. Scan the requirements table. Filter it to 'must' to see the core duties.
    3. Do. Note which of the reporting dates applies to your organisation first.
    4. Reflect. Which of the three capabilities is least developed in your organisation today?

    Check your understanding

    Which organisations must follow the policy?

    Show the answer

    UK government departments and their arm's length bodies. It applies to UK government departments and their ALBs. It is optional for public corporations and does not apply to councils.

    What must every identified data asset have?

    Show the answer

    A named data owner and a DQAP or equivalent. The policy requires a named data owner and a data quality action plan, or an equivalent mechanism, for every identified asset.

    M02 · Foundation · 15 minutes

    Roles and accountability

    Governance
    Activities done0 of 4 done

    By the end you will be able to

    • Describe the data owner, data steward and data custodian roles
    • Explain how the data owner relates to the information asset owner
    • Choose between the three ownership options

    Activities

    1. Read. Read the role model shared by all three playbooks.
    2. Read. Read the Assign recipe and its checklist.
    3. Do. List your critical data assets and the role that should own each one.
    4. Reflect. Does every owner in your list have a named successor?

    Check your understanding

    Which role is accountable for an asset's meaning, content and quality?

    Show the answer

    Data owner. The data owner is the senior leader accountable. The steward runs the asset day to day; the custodian runs the technical system.

    What must happen when a data owner leaves?

    Show the answer

    A successor is ready and the metadata is updated. The guidance asks you to tell the coordinating team, have a successor ready and update the metadata.

    M03 · Practitioner · 30 minutes

    Setting up governance with five recipes

    Governance
    Activities done0 of 4 done

    By the end you will be able to

    • Run the Mandate, Frame, Assign, Operate and Assure recipes in order
    • Set up a decision log and an exception register
    • Collect evidence continuously for the GDS return

    Activities

    1. Read. Work through the five recipes step by step.
    2. Do. Draft a one-page charter using the template.
    3. Do. Set up a decision log and an exception register using the templates.
    4. Reflect. Where will your evidence folder live, and who keeps it current?

    Check your understanding

    What makes an exception different from a policy change?

    Show the answer

    It has an owner and an expiry date. An exception is time-bound with an owner and an expiry date. Without an expiry it is an undocumented policy change.

    When should you assemble evidence for the return?

    Show the answer

    Continuously during Operate. Collecting evidence each time a decision is logged turns the return into collation rather than reconstruction.

    M04 · Practitioner · 25 minutes

    Finding and designating critical data assets

    Cataloguing
    Activities done0 of 4 done

    By the end you will be able to

    • Build a candidate list from existing registers
    • Apply the scope tests and the seven purposes
    • Draw an asset's boundary and record why

    Activities

    1. Read. Read stages 1 to 3 of the cataloguing method.
    2. Do. Start a candidate log using the designation template.
    3. Do. For one candidate, record which of the seven purposes it supports.
    4. Reflect. Have you looked at data you acquire from others, not only data you provide?

    Check your understanding

    If you designate a distribution of a data set, what else should you designate?

    Show the answer

    Its source data set. The guidance asks you to designate the source data set as well.

    Which of these is one of the seven purposes?

    Show the answer

    Official statistics. Official statistics is one of the seven. The statistics themselves are not designated, only the data used to produce them.

    M05 · Practitioner · 30 minutes

    Describing assets with metadata others can use

    Cataloguing
    Activities done0 of 4 done

    By the end you will be able to

    • Record the metadata properties in the GOV.UK guidance
    • Write titles and descriptions in plain English
    • Map properties to the Metadata Exchange Model

    Activities

    1. Read. Read the metadata properties and how to record each one.
    2. Do. Complete a catalogue record for one asset using the template.
    3. Do. Ask someone outside your team to read the record and say what the data is for.
    4. Reflect. Which properties did you have to mark 'unknown'? Who could supply them?

    Check your understanding

    How should you record a date in a catalogue record?

    Show the answer

    In ISO 8601 format. The guidance asks for ISO 8601, for example 2020-07-14.

    What should you put when a property has no value?

    Show the answer

    'unknown' or 'not applicable'. Use 'unknown' or 'not applicable' rather than leaving a blank.

    M06 · Practitioner · 20 minutes

    Cataloguing, harvesting and reporting to GDS

    Cataloguing
    Activities done0 of 3 done

    By the end you will be able to

    • Expose records so others can harvest them
    • Produce the return from catalogue records
    • Plan the annual review

    Activities

    1. Read. Read stages 6 to 8 of the cataloguing method.
    2. Do. Check that every link in one record works from outside your network.
    3. Do. Put the annual review date in the same diary entry as the DQAP review.

    Check your understanding

    How often must metadata be reviewed?

    Show the answer

    At least every 12 months. The policy asks for metadata to be maintained and reviewed annually.

    Who assures the returns of arm's length bodies?

    Show the answer

    Their department. Departments coordinate and assure the returns of their ALBs.

    M07 · Foundation · 15 minutes

    Data quality foundations

    Quality
    Activities done0 of 3 done

    By the end you will be able to

    • Name the five principles and six dimensions of the Government Data Quality Framework
    • Explain what a DQAP is for
    • Recognise when quality differs between groups of people

    Activities

    1. Read. Read the principles and dimensions.
    2. Read. Read the overview of the quality playbook.
    3. Reflect. Which dimension matters most for your most important decision?

    Check your understanding

    Which is one of the six dimensions?

    Show the answer

    Timeliness. The six are completeness, uniqueness, consistency, timeliness, validity and accuracy.

    Why break quality results down by group?

    Show the answer

    So poor quality for some people is not averaged away. An average can hide poor quality for some groups, which can exclude them from services.

    M08 · Practitioner · 35 minutes

    Building a DQAP and scoring issues

    Quality
    Activities done0 of 4 done

    By the end you will be able to

    • Write rules with targets from the issues framework
    • Measure a baseline and log issues
    • Prioritise issues and set resolution dates

    Activities

    1. Read. Work through the eight quality stages step by step.
    2. Do. Write three rules for one critical data element using the template.
    3. Do. Score an issue with the issues framework calculator.
    4. Reflect. Which issue would you fix at source rather than clean downstream?

    Check your understanding

    How does the issues framework set a rule's target range?

    Show the answer

    From importance of purpose times impact. Importance of purpose multiplied by impact gives a combined score, which sets the target range.

    In the framework's example timescales, when is a critical issue resolved?

    Show the answer

    Immediately. The framework's example gives Critical as immediate; High, Medium and Low within 1, 3 and 6 measurements.

    M09 · Advanced · 20 minutes

    Running the three capabilities as one yearly cycle

    Governance Cataloguing Quality
    Activities done0 of 3 done

    By the end you will be able to

    • Use the hand-offs so each fact is recorded once
    • Plan one annual review per asset
    • Feed assurance lessons into the next mandate

    Activities

    1. Read. Read the hand-offs between the capabilities.
    2. Read. Read the joined-up year.
    3. Do. Pick one asset and check the owner is the same in the register, the record and the DQAP.

    Check your understanding

    Where should the owner and successor for an asset be recorded first?

    Show the answer

    In the ownership register, then reused. The ownership register is the source; the catalogue record and the DQAP reuse it (IL-01, IL-02).

    M10 · Foundation · 20 minutes

    Inclusive data practice

    Governance Cataloguing Quality
    Activities done0 of 3 done

    By the end you will be able to

    • Apply equality and inclusion checks in each capability
    • Make data quality statements and records accessible
    • Involve people the data describes

    Activities

    1. Read. Read the inclusion checks for each capability.
    2. Do. Run the inclusion questions against one of your assets.
    3. Reflect. Whose voice is missing when you decide what 'good enough' means?

    Check your understanding

    Which duty asks public bodies to consider equality in their work?

    Show the answer

    The public sector equality duty. The public sector equality duty in the Equality Act 2010 applies to public authorities in their functions.

    M11 · Practitioner · 30 minutes

    Embedding the policy in delivery and change

    Governance Cataloguing Quality
    Activities done0 of 4 done

    By the end you will be able to

    • Name the capabilities the policy relies on but does not state
    • Build DAM checkpoints into a business case and an assurance plan
    • Plan a legacy exit and a handover to business as usual

    Activities

    1. Read. Read what the policy leaves to you and the life cycle overlay.
    2. Do. Take one programme you know and tick the actions that already happen, then list the gaps.
    3. Do. Run the self-assessment for your organisation and download the result.
    4. Reflect. Which critical asset would be left without an owner if its project closed tomorrow?

    Check your understanding

    Which guidance makes a register of data assets its second principle?

    Show the answer

    Managing legacy technology. The GOV.UK guidance on managing legacy technology asks organisations to build a complete and accurate register of their data assets.

    How does the capability model flag a need the DAM Policy text does not state at all?

    Show the answer

    Inferred. Inferred means the need is not in the text and comes from good practice and adjacent rulebooks.
