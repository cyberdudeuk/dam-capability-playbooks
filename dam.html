    The spine of the playbooks Level: Foundation

    What the DAM Policy requires

    What the data asset management policy in government and its linked guidance require, which capability meets each requirement, and which step does the work.

    In brief

    • The policy applies to UK government departments and their arm's length bodies. It is optional for public corporations and does not apply to councils.
    • In-scope assets are classified OFFICIAL, critical across government and within your own ownership.
    • You must catalogue and report them, review the records every year, name an owner for each and have a data quality action plan.
    Easy read summary
    • The Data Asset Management Policy says how government must look after its most important data.
    • This page lists every thing the policy asks for.
    • Next to each one, it shows the step that helps you do it.
    4'must' requirements met by data governance
    4'must' requirements met by data cataloguing
    1'must' requirements met by data quality

    Who it applies to and what is in scope

    Who. UK government departments and their arm's length bodies, including executive agencies, non-departmental public bodies and non-ministerial departments. It is optional for public corporations and does not apply to councils. The devolved governments have their own data sharing governanceV: Verified against the primary source on 2026-11-05.

    What. Identify the data assets in scope: classified OFFICIAL (including handling markings such as 'personal'), meeting the critical data asset criteria, and inside your own data ownership accountabilities.

    Requirements

    'Must' and 'should' follow the wording of the source. 'Method' is a prescribed technique. 'Gap' means the source promises something it does not yet set out.
