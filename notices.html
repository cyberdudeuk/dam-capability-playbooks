    Disclaimers, credits and citations

    Disclaimers and transparency notices

    Not official guidance

    This portal is a prototype. It is not an official GOV.UK service, is not published or endorsed by GDS or any department, and does not change what the DAM Policy requires. Where it repeats GDS guidance, the GOV.UK page is the authority.

    Not legal advice

    Nothing here is legal advice. For questions about data protection, data sharing powers or equality duties, ask your data protection officer or legal adviser.

    Accuracy and currency

    Facts carry a provenance label and sources are re-checked weekly. Guidance changes; check the date on each source and the Status page before relying on a detail.

    Information above OFFICIAL

    Do not enter information classified above OFFICIAL, or personal data, into this portal, its forms or its GitHub repository. Everything submitted through GitHub is public.

    Use of artificial intelligence

    An AI assistant (Claude, by Anthropic) helped research, write and build this portal. A person directed the work and reviews each release. Automated checks and source verification are described on the About page.

    External links

    Links to other sites are provided for reference. We are not responsible for their content or availability; the weekly health run reports links that stop working.

    Re-use

    You can re-use the content under the Open Government Licence v3.0 and the code under the MIT licence. Third-party material keeps its own licence, listed under credits.

    Cookies and tracking

    The portal sets no cookies and uses no analytics. Progress and display choices are kept in your own browser and can be cleared at any time.

    Attribution

    Contains public sector information licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0. Material quoted or summarised from GOV.UK is Crown copyright. Re-use it under the Open Government Licence v3.0.
