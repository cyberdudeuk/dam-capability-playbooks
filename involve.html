    Get involved

    This portal is built in the open, and anyone can help: use it and tell us what works, test it, suggest and vote for ideas, improve the content, review it as an expert, or champion it in your own team. Every way of taking part is recognised with a badge.

    • 9 ways to take part, from 2 minutes to an ongoing role.
    • A toolkit of emails, posts and a walk-through to bring your team in.
    • 6 badges for your profile and signature.

    Rules of engagement

    • Follow the code of conduct. Be kind, assume good faith and stay on topic.
    • Nothing above OFFICIAL, no personal data, and no email addresses anywhere.
    • Name roles, not people. Credit is given by GitHub username, only if you choose.
    • Every fact needs a source and a provenance label (see How sources are labelled).
    • Anyone can suggest a change. The owner reviews every change before it is published, and nobody is added as a collaborator except by the owner's invitation.
    • Decisions are made in the open, through the roadmap and the decisions log on Have your say.

    Improve the content

    1. Small fix or question: use "Report a problem with this page" at the foot of any page, or the contact form.
    2. Change a page: select "Suggest a change" at the foot of a page. GitHub makes your own copy, you edit the content file, and you open a pull request. The owner reviews it.
    3. New topic or sector supplement: post it as an idea first, so others can add evidence.
    4. Check a capability flag: on the capability model, select "Check this flag" on any capability.

    Read the contributing guide and code of conduct first.

    Champions and ambassadors

    Champions bring the portal into their own teams; ambassadors carry it across organisations. About 1 to 2 hours a month for a champion. Stop at any time.

    What a champion does

    • Share the portal with your team using the toolkit below
    • Run a 15 minute walk-through at a team meeting
    • Collect your team's feedback and ideas and post them
    • Encourage colleagues to take part in research
    • Tell us when your organisation's practice differs from the portal, so we can fix it

    What you get

    • Early sight of what is coming on the roadmap
    • A Champion badge for your GitHub profile and email signature
    • Your organisation's needs reflected in the roadmap
    • Recognition by GitHub username on the portal if you choose

    What an ambassador does

    • Everything a champion does
    • Present the portal at a community, network or working group
    • Bring back what other organisations need
    • Help shape the roadmap at a review panel

    Intranet news item

    Headline: Help shape practical guidance for the Data Asset Management Policy

The DAM capability playbooks are a prototype website that helps organisations meet the Data Asset Management Policy. It sets out the steps for each role in data governance, data cataloguing and data quality, with templates, a capability model and links to GOV.UK guidance.

The team is looking for people to test it, take part in research, and suggest ideas. Find out more: [this portal's address]/research.html

It is not official GOV.UK guidance. Where it repeats GOV.UK, the GOV.UK page is the authority.

    Badges

    Every way of taking part earns a badge you can show on your GitHub profile, on social media or in your email signature. Badges recognise help with a prototype. They are not an official government credential or qualification.

    Badges and how to earn them
    BadgeHow to earn itHow to get it

    Explorer    		Used the portal for real work and gave feedback.Self-declared: display it once you have done it.

    Tester    		Completed at least three testing tasks, or took part in a research session.Self-declared: display it once you have done it.

    Contributor    		An idea, report or change of yours was accepted onto the backlog or published.Claim it, with a link to the idea

    Reviewer    		Reviewed a draft as part of the review panel or an expert review.Claim it, with confirmed by the service owner.

    Champion    		Introduced the portal to your team and ran a walk-through or passed on your team's feedback.Claim it, with a short note of what you did

    Ambassador    		Presented the portal at a community, network or working group, or championed it across organisations.Claim it, with the community or event

    Claims are public GitHub issues that show your GitHub username only. Do not include your name or anything above OFFICIAL. No GitHub account? Use the private feedback form and say which badge.
