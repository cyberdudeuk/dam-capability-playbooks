    Data asset management (DAM) policy Level: Foundation

    Data Asset Management (DAM) capability playbooks

    Practical guidance for people who govern, catalogue and manage the quality of critical data assets in UK government departments and arm's length bodies.

    In brief

    • The DAM Policy asks every department and arm's length body to identify, describe, own and manage the quality of its critical data assets.
    • Three capabilities make that possible: data governance, data cataloguing and data quality. This portal puts them side by side.
    • It is more detailed than GOV.UK guidance and written for specialists. GOV.UK remains the authority on what the policy requires.
    Easy read summary
    • This website helps people follow the Data Asset Management Policy.
    • The policy is about looking after important government data.
    • It shows the steps for your role.
    • It is a test version. It is not official GOV.UK guidance.

    The three capabilities

    Data governance

    Who decides, by what authority, and how do we know it is working?

    Put the authority, roles and routines in place so that every critical data asset has a named, active owner, decisions are made and recorded, and the organisation can evidence its DAM Policy adoption.

    Data cataloguing

    What critical data do we hold, is it described well enough to find and reuse, and have we told GDS?

    Find the critical data assets, describe each one to the cross-government metadata standard, keep the descriptions in a catalogue that others can harvest, and report them to GDS every year.

    Steps done0 of 8 done

    Start the step by step

    Data quality

    Is the data good enough for what it is used for, and are we fixing what is not?

    Know, prove and improve the quality of each critical data asset through a data quality action plan (DQAP), scored with the government issues framework, reported openly and repeated every cycle.
