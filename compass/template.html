            The DAMA wheel: from data management area to activity

            Start from a data management area on the DAMA wheel, drill down to the capabilities in it and the activities under each one, down to the individual steps, and see exactly where each is in the four portals.

            Areas come from the DAMA Data Management Body of Knowledge (DMBOK) as the DAM playbooks place them. Which recipe stage serves which capability, and the Commons, catalogue and DCAT-AP-UK links for each area, are this compass's own mappings. Change items, perspectives and maturity strands carry their own capability links from the DAM playbooks.

                    About this compass

                    A neutral guide to four separate, open projects. It was made with help from an AI assistant (Claude) and checked by automated tests; a person reviews each release. Facts about each portal come from that portal's own pages, read on __ASOF__. None is an official GOV.UK service. Where they repeat GOV.UK guidance, GOV.UK is the authority.
