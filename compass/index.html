Estate compass

Find your bearing across four portals for data in the public sector. Not sure which portal you need, or in what order? Answer four quick questions and the compass points the way, step by step, with why each step matters and what you get from it.

North is the policy, east is the practice, west is what already exists, south is how it is described so others can find it. You are at the centre.
