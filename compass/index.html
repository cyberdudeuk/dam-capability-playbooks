                      About this compass

                      A neutral guide to four separate, open projects. It was made with help from an AI assistant (Claude) and checked by automated tests; a person reviews each release. Facts about each website come from that portal's own pages, read on 2026-11-13. None is an official GOV.UK service. Where they repeat GOV.UK guidance, GOV.UK is the authority.
