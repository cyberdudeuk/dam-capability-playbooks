    Boards, councils and networks

    Who decides, who advises and where practitioners meet, for data asset management in UK government. Each entry says what the body is for, how it relates to the DAM Policy and how you can take part. Facts were checked on GOV.UK and data.gov.uk on 5 November 2026; where terms of reference are not published, it says so.

    How standards and decisions flow

    For data standards, practitioners develop a proposal in a community; the Peer Review Group reviews it; the DSA Steering Board endorses itV: Verified against the primary source on 2026-11-05.

    1. Communities of expertsSpot problems, share practice and help develop proposals
    2. Peer Review GroupReviews products, proposals and standards
    3. DSA Steering BoardEndorses cross-government standards (final step)

    Alongside this chain, the CDO Council sets strategy and weighs strategic risk, the Open Standards Board decides on open standards, and the architecture design authority decides on data architecture.

    Source: Data Standards Authority: operational model and processes.

    Boards and groups that decide or advise

    • Decision-making council · Government Digital Service

      Chief Data Officer (CDO) Council

      Purpose: The decision-making body overseeing strategic delivery of Mission 3 of the National Data Strategy (government's own use of data).V: Verified against the primary source on 2026-11-05

      Aims
      • Collective accountability for government's own use and reuse of data
      • Set future strategy as targets are met
      • Help the Data Standards Authority identify strategic risks, problems and opportunities for common standards
      Expected outcomes
      Strategic direction for data across government; it does not set specific data standards.
      Link to the DAM Policy
      DAM Policy artefacts such as the Data Ownership Model are presented to the CDO Council and its Executive before adoption.O: This playbook's own synthesis of published guidance
      How to take part
      Through your organisation's chief data officer or equivalent senior data leader. Raise issues with them; the council is not open to direct membership.
      Terms of reference
      Terms of reference not found published on GOV.UK.

    • Endorsement board · Government Digital Service

      Data Standards Authority (DSA) Steering Board

      Purpose: A senior advisory group that supports the Data Standards Authority in setting cross-government data standardisation strategy and delivery plans.V: Verified against the primary source on 2026-11-05

      Aims
      • Oversee the DSA's operations and priorities
      • Approve cross-government standards as the final step of endorsement
      • Drive implementation in member organisations
      • Monitor enforcement and assurance
      Expected outcomes
      Endorsed data standards for central government, and their implementation in member organisations.
      Link to the DAM Policy
      Endorsed the data quality action plan guidance and was presented the essential shared data assets policy and Data Ownership Model, which the DAM Policy builds on.O: This playbook's own synthesis of published guidance
      How to take part
      Through your organisation's representative. Minutes are published, so you can follow decisions.
      Terms of reference
      Role set out in the DSA operational model; minutes published.

    • Advisory group · Government Digital Service and the Office for National Statistics

      Peer Review Group for the DSA and the Data Quality Hub

      Purpose: Reviews products, proposals and standards before they go to the DSA Steering Board, and reviews products from the Government Data Quality Hub.V: Verified against the primary source on 2026-11-05

      Aims
      • Shape the DSA's approach and priorities
      • Help develop proposals, weighing cost, impact, skills and technical feasibility
      • Suggest priority areas for standardisation
      Expected outcomes
      Proposals that are tested by practitioners before endorsement.
      Link to the DAM Policy
      Reviews data quality and metadata products that the DAM Policy relies on.O: This playbook's own synthesis of published guidance
      How to take part
      Through your organisation's representative, or by bringing evidence to a community that feeds it.
      Terms of reference
      Role set out in the DSA operational model; minutes published.

    • Standards board · Cabinet Office (Government Digital Service)

      Open Standards Board

      Purpose: Accountable for the transparent selection and implementation of open standards for government.V: Verified against the primary source on 2026-11-05

      Aims
      • Consider open standards for software interoperability, data and document formats
      • Decide whether a standard is adopted or made compulsory
      • Make sure open standards are considered before government creates its own
      Expected outcomes
      Open standards that all of government must or should use, published with profiles on GOV.UK.
      Link to the DAM Policy
      The route for any data or metadata standard a DAM catalogue or tool should use; see Tools and interoperability.O: This playbook's own synthesis of published guidance
      How to take part
      Propose or support an open standards challenge; volunteer when members are recruited. Members serve 2 years and declare interests.
      Terms of reference
      Terms of reference on the group's GOV.UK page; terms and conditions for members published.

    • Design authority · Government Digital Service

      Data and Technology Architecture Design Authority (DTADA)

      Purpose: The decision-making body for data architecture principles, policies, strategies and standards.V: Verified against the primary source on 2026-11-05

      Aims
      • Commission and approve data architecture artefacts
      • Oversee the national reference data architecture
      • Approve domain conceptual data models
      • Coordinate with other design authorities
      Expected outcomes
      Joined-up data architecture across government. It is a sub-group of the CDO and CTO councils.
      Link to the DAM Policy
      Sets architecture that catalogues, data models and reference data used under the DAM Policy should follow.O: This playbook's own synthesis of published guidance
      How to take part
      Through your organisation's architecture lead.
      Terms of reference
      Role set out in the DSA operational model; terms of reference not found published.

      • Gather feedback on guidance
      Expected outcomes
      Departments supported to adopt the DAM Policy, and guidance shaped by practitioners.

      Join through the Knowledge Hub; the data.gov.uk page has the link.

      Not found published.

    Where to take an issue

    Where to take each kind of issue
    Your issueWhere to take it
    A question or a problem with a DAM Policy requirementYour DAM Policy coordinator, then the Data Management Community
    A data quality method or toolThe Government Data Quality Community
    A proposed data or metadata standardAn open standards challenge to the Open Standards Board, or the DSA through the Peer Review Group
    A data sharing blockerThe Data Sharing Network of Experts
    A data model, taxonomy or architecture questionThe Government Data Architecture Community and Taxonomy Oversight Group
    A strategic risk for your organisationYour chief data officer, who can take it to the CDO Council
