    Working together

    How data, records and information, security, digital, technology, analysis, commercial and delivery professionals can act on the same critical assets.

    • 10 recommendations
    • 15 communities
    • 8 professions

    In brief

    • Ten practical recommendations, each with an action, an outcome and a measure.
    • Communities and networks to join, filtered by role.
    • A map of which professions contribute to which capabilities.

    Recommendations

    Data asset management needs data, records and information, security, digital, technology, analysis, commercial and delivery professionals to act on the same assets. These recommendations turn that into shared artefacts, routines and networks, each with a tangible outcome you can check.O: This playbook's own synthesis of published guidance

    Recommendations done0 of 10 done
    • 01

      J-01

      Keep one register of assets

      Do: Reconcile the critical data asset list with the information asset register so each asset has one record, with the data owner and information asset owner linked.

      Outcome: One list used by data, records and security teams; no asset is reported twice or missed.

      Measure: Assets on both lists without a shared identifier: zero.

      Who: Lead policy coordinator Information asset owner Catalogue manager

    • 02

      J-02

      Use one glossary

      Do: Publish one organisation glossary for data, records and security terms, linked to the cross-government vocabulary, and point catalogue entries to it.

      Outcome: The same word means the same thing in a catalogue entry, a file plan and a risk register.

      Measure: Critical assets whose key items link to glossary terms.

      Who: Data steward Catalogue manager

    • 03

      J-03

      Run one board for data, information and security risk

      Do: Bring the data governance board, information assurance and the senior information risk owner together, quarterly, with one agenda covering critical assets.

      Outcome: Decisions on an asset consider data, records and security together.

      Measure: Decisions recorded per quarter that cite more than one discipline.

      Who: Chief data officer Data governance board Information asset owner

    • 04

      J-04

      Put DAM checkpoints in every programme

      Do: Agree with the programme office a standard set of DAM checks for business cases, gates and handover, and give programmes a named data contact.

      Outcome: Programmes stop creating unowned, uncatalogued assets.

      Measure: Programmes closing with every in-scope asset owned, catalogued and quality-planned.

      Who: Chief data officer Programme office and assurance Senior responsible owner

    • 05

      J-05

      Align retention with the data life cycle

      Do: Map retention schedules to critical assets and record retention and disposal in catalogue entries.

      Outcome: Data is kept as long as needed and no longer, with evidence.

      Measure: Critical assets with a recorded retention rule.

      Who: Information asset owner Catalogue manager Data owner

    • 06

      J-06

      Learn together

      Do: Run a shared induction for owners, stewards, information asset owners and programme leads, using the recommended curriculum and this portal's modules.

      Outcome: People in different professions use the same method and language.

      Measure: Owners and stewards who completed the shared induction.

      Who: Chief data officer Lead policy coordinator

    • 07

      J-07

      Join the right communities

      Do: Give each role one community to join and one to watch, from the list on this page, and share what you learn at the board.

      Outcome: Practice spreads across organisations instead of being reinvented.

      Measure: Roles with a named community; lessons shared per quarter.

      Who: Lead policy coordinator Data steward Data quality lead or analyst Catalogue manager

    • 08

      J-08

      Rehearse an incident together

      Do: Run a joint exercise where a critical asset is corrupted or exposed, involving security, records, data quality and the owner.

      Outcome: Everyone knows their part in recovering and re-trusting an asset.

      Measure: Critical assets covered by a tested recovery and quality check.

      Who: Data owner Data custodian Data quality lead or analyst Data protection officer

    • 09

      J-09

      Share contract clauses

      Do: Keep a shared library of data, metadata, quality and exit clauses with the commercial team and reuse it in every relevant procurement.

      Outcome: Suppliers face the same data obligations everywhere.

      Measure: New contracts touching critical assets that use the shared clauses.

      Who: Commercial lead Data owner Data protection officer

    • 10

      J-10

      Report one set of measures

      Do: Combine catalogue completeness, DQAP status, records compliance and security assurance for critical assets into one quarterly view.

      Outcome: Leaders see one picture of how well critical assets are managed.

      Measure: Quarterly view produced and discussed at the board.

      Who: Lead policy coordinator Data governance board
