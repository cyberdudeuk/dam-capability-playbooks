# Packs for backlog items that need people

Version 1.10.0, 5 November 2026. The build delivered every backlog item that software can deliver (BL-15 to BL-21, BL-26 to BL-28). The items below need a person, a group or an outside body. Each has what is ready, what is needed and who acts.

## BL-12 Private research sign-up form (Now)
- **Ready:** the questions, answer types and reasons are in the user research bundle (Claude Docs artifact "Have your say: user research bundle"), section "Sign-up form questions".
- **Needed:** build the form in Microsoft Forms or GOV.UK Forms; set "anyone can respond" and turn off "record name"; put its address in `research.signup.url` in `content/frameworks.yaml`. The build rejects any other host.
- **Who:** service owner.

## BL-13 Round 0: introduce and recruit (Now)
- **Ready:** emails 1 to 3, the one-page introduction and the owner checklist in the bundle; the champions toolkit on Get involved.
- **Needed:** send email 1 through the Data Management Community and the DAM Policy coordinators' network; triage sign-ups weekly.
- **Who:** service owner, with champions.

## BL-22 Clearance and an official home (Now)
- **Decision needed:** whether this prototype becomes guidance departments may rely on.
- **Options:** (1) stay a practitioner prototype, clearly labelled, as now; (2) offer it to the GDS data management team as a candidate for the DAM Policy's supporting guidance, through the Data Management Community; (3) take it through the DSA Peer Review Group to the DSA Steering Board for endorsement of specific artefacts (for example the capability model or the sector quality rules).
- **What a reviewer will ask:** sources and provenance (every fact labelled), accessibility (WCAG 2.2 AA checks on every release), security (no server, strict content security policy, reviewed actions), licences (OGL v3.0 content, MIT code) and ownership (a named team to keep it current).
- **Move to an official GitHub organisation:** transfer the repository only when an owning team agrees; keep the owner-only collaborator rule.
- **Who:** service owner, then the GDS data management team.

- **Needed:** 5 to 8 participants per role from Round 0, including assistive technology users; a note-taker; publish findings under "What we have learned".

## BL-14 Expert review of the sector supplements (Next)
- **Invitation:** "We would value an hour of your time to check the [sector] supplement on the DAM capability playbooks: are the governance overlays, catalogue fields and quality rules right for [sector]? Please mark what is wrong, missing or out of date. You will be credited as a Reviewer by GitHub username if you wish."
- **Review checklist:** each rule's dimension and threshold is right; each catalogue field matches the sector's own standards; each governance overlay names the right legal basis; nothing is above OFFICIAL.
- **Where reviewers come from:** the networks on Boards, councils and networks (for example NHS and social care data communities, the Data Linkage Champions Network, Government Statistical Service quality champions).
