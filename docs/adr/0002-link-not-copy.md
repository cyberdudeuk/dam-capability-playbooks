# ADR 0002: Link to masters, never copy them

Date: 2026-11-05. Status: accepted.

## Context
The portfolio baseline records that playbooks are "published, none joined". A new portal risks becoming one more copy.

## Decision
Where a master exists (GOV.UK guidance, the Metadata Exchange Model, the Data Quality Commons tools), the portal links to it
and summarises only what is needed to join the three capabilities. It adds what no master holds: the requirements spine,
one role model, the hand-offs and one annual cycle.

## Consequences
The portal stays small and cannot contradict a master for long. When a master changes, the review date and the
verified-on date on each source show what to recheck.
